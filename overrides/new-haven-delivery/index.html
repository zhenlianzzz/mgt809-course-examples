Lecture 6 · Evolutionary optimization

New Haven delivery planning

One van leaves the Long Wharf kitchen, delivers lunch to 12 locations, and returns. Choose the order of the 12 visits, see the travel time change, then build the same decision in Excel.

Minimize total driving time.

Visit every delivery stop exactly once, starting and ending at the kitchen. The decision variables are the stops assigned to visit positions 1–12. Try different sequences below, then use the travel-time matrix and Evolutionary Solver in Excel.

A fast route can still be expensive.

Leave at 11:30 AM and allow 2 minutes of unloading at each stop. A late arrival triggers that stop’s full refund. Extend your Excel model to minimize driver cost + mileage cost + refunds.

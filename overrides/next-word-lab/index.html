What happens when I click a training button?
  1. The model predicts probabilities for all seven sentences.
  2. It calculates average prediction loss: −ln(probability of the correct word).
  3. It calculates how each weight affects the loss, then updates the weights.

The formulas and training sentences stay fixed. All displayed predictions come from the current weights.

This is the same small, 136-weight model and loss as the Excel exercise. The browser computes gradients through backpropagation and uses Adam updates at a learning rate of 0.03, with weights bounded between −5 and 5. Excel uses GRG Nonlinear. Individual updates can temporarily increase loss.

The seven examples are also the training data; this demo illustrates fitting them. It does not test knowledge of unseen countries. Transformer architecture reference.
